Click Start, allow your microphone, and talk. Interrupt it whenever you like.

## Features

- 🎙️ **Two broadcasts**: your browser publishes your microphone with [@moq/publish](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/js/@moq/publish) and plays the agent's reply with [@moq/watch](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/js/@moq/watch). No WebRTC, no SFU.
- 🤖 **Pipecat**: the agent is a [Pipecat](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat) pipeline speaking MoQ instead of WebRTC.
- ⏩ **Faster than real-time**: the agent sends its reply as fast as it's generated, seconds ahead of playback, so a congested network delays nothing you can hear.
- ✋ **Barge-in**: talk over the agent and it stops. The audio it already sent is retired with its track, not played out.
- 💬 **Transcripts**: both sides' words travel as a JSON track beside the audio, the same way [MoQ Boy](/boy) sends its controls.
