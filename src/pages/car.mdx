# CARLA

<Car />

Click a vehicle to watch its cameras and take control. Use **W/S** for throttle and brake,
**A/D** to steer, and **Space** to change gear. **E** stops the vehicle, **R** resets
the stop, and **Esc** releases control. A steering wheel works too.

The vehicles run in [CARLA](https://carla.org) on a workstation. When it is offline,
the player says so.

## How it works

- **Video on demand.** Hover a vehicle for a 240p preview; select it for 720p.
  Cameras render and encode only while someone watches.
- **Control and telemetry.** Steering commands and vehicle status use JSON tracks
  on the same MoQ connection as the video. One driver holds control at a time;
  releasing it returns the vehicle to autonomy.
- **A shared world.** The vehicles can see each other, and their positions appear
  on the map even while their cameras are idle.

The [demo source and setup](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/tree/main/demo/carla)
include the player, simulator worker, and GPU video bridge.
