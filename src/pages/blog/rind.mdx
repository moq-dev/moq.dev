# Encrypting Vibe Slop
I'm one of the OG "rewrite it in Rust" guys.
**I love Rust**, but I wrote my last line of Rust 6 months ago.
Hell, I read my last line of Rust 3 months ago.

The consensus **worst part about Rust** is the **compile time**.
I think **async** is worse, but `target` is a close second.
My desktop PC was on fire even with [mbx](https://mr-boxington.jdx.dev/) (highly recommend).

~I~ Claude traced some of it down to `aws-lc-rs` taking 60s+ to build.
Why does a single crate take a full minute to build?

Despite the suffix, `aws-lc-rs` is actually a `C` library in disguise.
Same with the next best crypto library: `ring`.

wtf is C doing in my Rust library.
**THIS WILL NOT STAND.**

I did what any sane software engineer does in `current_year`,
I pulled a 9-year old gaming PC out of the closet, installed Claude, and ran `/goal rewrite it in rust`.

	## Optimize EVERYTHING
	Having accomplished my goal of making MoQ faster, I let my old gaming PC take a vacation.
	
	lul jk I ran: **/goal**: Support every algorithm, and make it the fastest.
	
	<figure>

	AI is **really fucking good** at benchmarks.
	It's terrifying.
	`rind` only loses on a few benchmarks, almost all for security reasons: on Apple's M4 it turns on DIT (constant-time mode) around every call.
	Hold DIT across a batch of calls, as MoQ does, and all but one of those losses go away.
	
	Moments like this fill me with dread as a professional software engineer.
	I **wasted weeks** speeding up some Go crypto library (contributor btw) when I could have just waited a decade for Opus 5.5 to release instead.
