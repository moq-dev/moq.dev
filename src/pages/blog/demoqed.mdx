# DE MOQ ED

[Demuxed](https://www.demuxed.com/) is the best media conference.
It happens once a year in San Francisco.
Except for that one time in London but we don't talk about that.

**Demuxed 2026 is cancelled.**

AI companies booked all of the big venues in San Francisco.
So we're hosting [DEMOQED](https://moqalliance.org/demoqed-2026) instead.

## WHEN
October 8th, 2026. 1pm - 8pm

## WHERE
San Francisco @ [Alamo Draft House](https://share.google/DPkfeck6IG5HjEPpf)

It's during [Tech Week](https://www.tech-week.com/) so convince your boss to let you attend.
[HLS interest](https://developer.apple.com/events/view/3ZNQ5DGP99/dashboard) and FOMs are on the same week.

## WHO
[Moq Alliance](https://moqalliance.org/) is hosting the event.
We be a collection of companies implementing MoQ that want it to succeed.

If you want to join **THE ALLIANCE**, help with the event, or sponsor... let us know.
There's a [Discord server](https://discord.gg/neXqzCXsgD) for you zoomers and an [email address](mailto:hello@moqalliance.org) for you boomers.

## WHAT
We'll have a bunch of talks and demos and discussions and IDK.

[Send a mail](mailto:hello@moqalliance.org?subject=DEMOQED%202026%20talk%20submission) if you want to give a talk.
Please no sales pitches. You gotta actually be building something MoQ related. 

We can't replace the pomp and prestige of Demuxed, but we'll do **SOMETHING**.
If you have any ideas or want to sponsor something, **REACH OUT**


## WHY
The IETF is a great venue for arguing about the MoQ wire encoding.
You can fly to their IRL meetings every 2 months if you're a landscaper and want to get in the weeds.

But we want to focus on the practical applications of MoQ.
Talk about how we're building real shit.
Get to know your neighbors, maybe collaborate on something, etc.

And like yeah didn't you hear?
There's no Demuxed this year.
