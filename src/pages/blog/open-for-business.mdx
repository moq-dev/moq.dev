## The Standard
[moq-transport](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-moq-transport/) will be an RFC one day.
There's quite a few companies working on their own CDN offering: [Cloudflare](https://moq.dev/blog/first-cdn/), Akamai, Fastly, AWS, etc.
But progress is *so slow*; it's embarassing.

Hopefully this gives the group a kick in the butt.
Stop arguing about how to encode an integer and actually charge for something.
We don't need more tech demos.

All of the [moq.dev](https://moq.dev) clients are fully compatible with the standard, supporting drafts 14-19.
You should be able to switch out the `https://cdn.moq.pro` URL with a different CDN provider (ex. [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/moq-relays/))

The [moq.pro](https://moq.pro) relay doesn't support every feature in the IETF standard.
To be fair, zero relays fully support the standard, owing to how bloated and *optional* it is.
Hit me up if you want to use a 3rd party MoQ client that isn't compatible and we'll figure out a path forward.

## Try it!
**Fun fact:** here's a *few* of the ways to watch MoQ broadcasts:

- [Web](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/js/@moq/watch.html): https://moq.watch/demo/bbb.hang
- [CLI](https://doc.moq.dev/bin/cli.html): `moq --client-connect "https://cdn.moq.pro/demo/bbb.hang" export ts | ffplay -`
- [RTMP](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/tree/main/rs/moq-rtmp): `rtmps://cdn.moq.pro:1935/demo/bbb.hang`
- [SRT](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/tree/main/rs/moq-srt): `srt://cdn.moq.pro:877?latency=500&streamid=#!::r=demo/bbb.hang,m=request`
- [WebRTC](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/tree/main/rs/moq-rtc): `https://cdn.moq.pro/whep/demo/bbb.hang`
- [Gstreamer](https://doc.moq.dev/bin/gstreamer.html#gstreamer-plugin): `gst-launch-1.0 -v -e moqsrc name=s url=https://cdn.moq.pro/demo broadcast=bbb.hang`
- [OBS](https://doc.moq.dev/bin/obs.html#obs-plugin): Create a MoQ source for `https://cdn.moq.pro/demo/bbb.hang`

And just a reminder that there are libraries for [Rust](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/rs/), [Typescript](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/js/), [C](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/rs/), [Python](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/py/), [Go](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/go/), [Swift](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/swift/), and [Kotlin](https://doc.moq.dev/lib/kt/).
